**Hardening suggestions** (optional, to increase depth of coverage):

- **Reasonableness**: Add absolute bounds: `val BETWEEN -1e15 AND 1e15`
- **Freshness**: Add timestamp recency: `ingested_at <= CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`
- **Volume**: Add row count bounds per CIK: flag CIKs with >2x median row count
- **Referential Integrity**: Add CIK existence check against `base.entity_mappings`
